Elon Musk is a technology entrepreneur and business leader known for founding and leading several companies. He is the CEO of Tesla, an electric vehicle and clean energy company, and the founder and CEO of SpaceX, which develops rockets and spacecraft. Musk has also been involved with companies such as Neuralink, which focuses on brain-computer interfaces, and The Boring Company, which develops tunnel-based transportation infrastructure. He acquired Twitter in 2022 and later renamed it X. Musk was born in Pretoria, South Africa, and later moved to Canada and the United States. His companies operate in areas including electric vehicles, space exploration, artificial intelligence, robotics, and transportation.
